Key Findings
Market Structure
Total ETF AUM: Comprehensive analysis of assets under management

Market Concentration: Detailed HHI analysis revealing market competition levels

Top Fund Managers: Identification of dominant players and their market share

Size Distribution: Analysis of fund sizes from micro to large-cap ETFs

Investment Insights
Asset Allocation: Trends in equity, bond, and money market allocations

Cost Efficiency: Expense ratio analysis across different fund categories

Performance Metrics: Investment efficiency and operational effectiveness

Market Gaps: Identification of underserved segments and opportunities

Tech Stack
Python + Pandas for data analysis and manipulation

Jupyter Notebooks for reproducible research workflow

Matplotlib/Seaborn for professional visualizations and dashboards

NumPy/SciPy for statistical analysis and financial metrics

Git for version control and collaborative development

Data Sources
Nigerian Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) regulatory filings

Monthly ETF performance reports (2018-2025)

Fund manager disclosures and portfolio compositions

142 original Excel files processed and validated

Research Outputs
Cleaned Dataset: 257 validated ETF records with complete financial metrics

Market Analysis: Comprehensive Excel reports with multi-sheet analysis

Visual Dashboards: Professional charts and interactive visualizations

Executive Summary: Key insights and market intelligence

Research Roadmap: Future analysis opportunities and extensions
